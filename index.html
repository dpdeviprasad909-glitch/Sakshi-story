Where it all began

28 March 2022
Class 12

Neither of us probably knew that an ordinary day in Class 12 would eventually become the beginning of something that would mean this much to both of us.

We were just two classmates. Same room, same bells, same tired Mondays. Then came a few words here and there, a joke that landed, a seat that stopped feeling random. Somewhere along the way, without either of us announcing it, we became important parts of each other's days.

The little things I love about you

Your smile.

It has a way of changing the mood of a whole day, mine especially.

The way you care.

Quietly, properly, without needing to make a show of it.

The way you worry about me.

Even when I said I was fine, and you could tell I wasn't.

The way you put up with my roasts.

I teased you constantly. You never took it the wrong way, and your laugh back made it worth it.

The way you somehow know when something is wrong.

I never figured out how. You just knew.

The comfort I feel talking to you.

Ending a day by talking to you made the day feel finished in a good way.

The person you are when you're completely yourself.

Unfiltered, funny, a little stubborn, and really kind. That's the Sakshi I got to know.

We weren't perfect.

We had misunderstandings.
We had difficult days.
We hurt each other sometimes.
We disagreed.
We had moments where things felt heavy.

But somewhere between all of that were countless moments that made us laugh, feel understood, feel cared for and simply enjoy being around each other.

I don't want to pretend everything was perfect.

It wasn't.

But the beautiful parts were real.
And they will always mean something to me.

I'm sorry.

Sakshi,
I'm sorry.

I know I've hurt you. I know saying sorry doesn't erase what happened. I know I can't go back and change the moments where I should have acted differently.

I should have listened better. I should have understood you better. I should have been more thoughtful about how my actions affected you.

I'm not writing this to make excuses. I'm writing it because you deserve to hear me acknowledge that I was wrong.

I'm learning from what happened. And I don't expect you to immediately forgive me. You don't owe me that.

I just genuinely wanted to say that I'm sorry.

You became more than just someone I loved.

You became my best friend.

My comfort.

My favourite person to tease.

The person I could talk to at the end of the day.

The person who cared for me in ways I sometimes didn't even realise.

you became home.

You mean the world to me, Sakshi.

No pressure. Just honesty.

I made this because there were things I wanted to say, but I didn't want to keep forcing conversations when you were already hurt.

You don't owe me an answer.

You don't even have to respond to this.

I just wanted you to know that I'm genuinely sorry, that I care about you deeply, and that I'm grateful for every beautiful moment we've shared.

Take care of yourself, Sakshi. ❤️
